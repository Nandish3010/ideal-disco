# Synthetic evaluation clips

Synthetic voices, not field recordings; replaced by Nandish's recordings when available.

Generated by `scripts/make_eval_clips.py` with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech: the 10 scripts in `../labels.template.json` read by rotating en-IN voices at speaking rates 0.95 to 1.1, plus Kannada-English mixed versions of clip01 and clip05 (`clip01-kn.mp3`, `clip05-kn.mp3`, kn-IN voice). `labels.json` is the template plus those two clips; voice and rate per clip are recorded in it. Results of one pass against the deployed API are in `results.json`.

Run: `python3 scripts/eval_run.py --clips data/eval/synthetic --labels data/eval/synthetic/labels.json`
